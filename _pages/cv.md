### Selected administrative achievements

**Interim Associate Dean for Undergraduate Experience & Strategic Initiatives, College of Arts and Sciences (2023–2024).** Oversaw 14 General Faculty serving as Association Deans; developed processes for annual review, promotion rubrics, and governance. Led teams responsible for undergraduate course-requirement policy, curriculum planning and enrollment forecasting, summer orientation redesign, and a new communications strategy. Scaled the first-year curricular experience for 3,000+ students and redesigned advising for 12,000+ undergraduates, including a 20-person transitional-advising model budgeted at $3M/year.

**Chair, Department of Politics (2010–2017).** Led the largest department in the College: 43 tenure-track faculty, 900 majors, ~75 graduate students. Managed a $4M salary budget plus $250K/year in discretionary funds. Created the department's annual peer-review process; rewrote all promotion and tenure procedures; oversaw RPT for 16 faculty; built MOUs for joint appointments; created the Faculty Guides mentoring program. Led the redesign of undergraduate requirements; implemented a new graduate track in political methodology; raised funds for the Power, Violence, and Inequality and the Race and Politics programs. Founded the Department of Politics Alumni Advisory Board.

**Founding Director, Global Policy Center, Batten School (2015–2023).** Hired two Professors of Practice and administrative staff; recruited and managed 20+ affiliates. Built the Center's strategic focus on humanitarian policy (displacement, early childhood development, data analytics). Raised over $1.5M in contracts and grants on a $40K/year operating budget; employs 20+ undergraduate, MA, and PhD students annually.

**Director, Miller Center Policy Research Program (2017–present).** Created and directs the Center's Senior Fellows Program, enhancing the demographic, academic, and political diversity of the Center. Recruits and manages 40+ Faculty Senior Fellows, 10+ non-resident Senior Faculty Fellows, and 12 Practitioner Senior Fellows.
