I am a political economist at the University of Virginia, where I study the politics of international money, migration, and trade. My recent work examines how democratic institutions shape monetary credibility and the adoption of new financial technologies, the political and economic drivers of forced displacement, and the design of US trade policy.

This site collects my published work, working papers, talks, and teaching materials. Replication materials for published papers are linked from each [publication](/publications/).
